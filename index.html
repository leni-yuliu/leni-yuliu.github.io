Biography

I am currently a Ph.D. candidate at Faculty of Medicine, The Chinese University of Hong Kong. Previously, I received M.Phil. degree from Biomedical Engineering at Beihang University in 2021, and B.Eng. degree(Honor) from Biomedical Engineering at Hebei University of Technology in 2018.

My research interests include image computing, signal processing, computational fluid dynamics(CFD), and their application in neurology and neuroscience.

Recently, I focus on using CFD technique to investigate the hemodynamic patterns and clinical implications of intracranial atherosclerotic stenosis.
